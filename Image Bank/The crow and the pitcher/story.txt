Here is a 10-step retelling of the classic fable:

## The Crow and the Pitcher

1. **The Scorching Heat**
Under a blazing sun, a severe drought dried up all the streams, leaving a weary crow desperately searching for a single drop of water.
2. **A Glimmer of Hope**
Just as her strength began to fade, she spotted a tall glass pitcher standing on a farmyard table.
3. **The Frustrating Discovery**
Peering inside, she saw water at the bottom, but the narrow neck of the pitcher prevented her beak from reaching it.
4. **The First Failed Attempt**
She pushed her head down with all her force, but no matter how hard she tried, the water remained just out of touch.
5. **A Dangerous Risk**
The crow then tried to push the heavy pitcher over to spill the water, but it was far too sturdy to tip.
6. **Moment of Despair**
Exhausted and parched, she landed on the ground beside the table, fearing she would not survive the day.
7. **The Spark of an Idea**
Looking down, her eyes caught a pile of small pebbles scattered near the garden path, and a clever plan formed in her mind.
8. **Pebble by Pebble**
She picked up a pebble with her beak, flew up, and dropped it into the pitcher, watching it sink straight to the bottom.
9. **The Rising Tide**
Patiently, she carried pebble after pebble, dropping them in one by one until the rising stones slowly displaced the water, pushing it higher.
10. **Sweet Relief**
At last, the water reached the brim, allowing the clever crow to drink her fill and save her own life.

---

> **Moral:** Necessity is the mother of invention. Little by little, patience and clever thinking can solve the hardest problems.
